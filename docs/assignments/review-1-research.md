Saved from the Canvas assignment so an AI coach or a later session can find it. Canvas holds the official version.

- **Schedule:** introduced Nov 3 → work and review in class Nov 5 → submit in Canvas.
- **Rubric:** Complete, Thoughtful, Checked, Understood, 25 points each. ([rubric](https://kaw393939.github.io/is117-demo-days/course/milestones.html#how-each-step-is-graded))

## What to do

- Compare three relevant clothing sites: source/date, observation, and what it suggests.
- Compare two positions and two short persona hypotheses; choose an audience.
- Note look, brand character, and honest persuasion. Reuse Brand Studio and label assumptions.

## In class

A classmate questions one observation and explains your audience back to you. Record useful feedback in a project issue, respond, and revise or explain your decision.

## Submit in Canvas (text box)

The Research record link, relevant repository/site links, the feedback issue, and a short change/open-question note. A GitHub issue alone does not submit the work.
