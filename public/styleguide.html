00 / Direction

Who and why.

Full reasoning lives in SPEC.md.

Audience is an untested hypothesis

Audience

Student volunteers

New Jersey college students who help neighbors in need and want to feel part of a crew, without bragging.

Archetype

Everyperson

Down-to-earth and open to everyone who shows up.

Visual direction

Postmodern

After Martine Bedin’s Super lamp (Memphis, 1981): many colors on one shared base, mixed type, tilted cards.

06 / Voice

Impactful, local, connecting.

Short lines. Name Jersey. Invite people in.

Headline

“Pull it on, join the cause.”

Impactful: two short commands, no claims.

Shared identity (unity)

“From Jersey, for Jersey.”

Local and connecting: names the group the visitor already belongs to. No member counts or reviews.

Free tip (reciprocity)

“First shift? Do this.”

Useful advice whether or not anyone buys, labeled as general advice.

Avoid

Made-up history, impact numbers, reviews, “only 3 left”, or any fabric, fit or shipping claim we can’t support.

A living reference. Compare this board with the storefront at the same width: logo, headline and body type, main button, colors and voice should match.
